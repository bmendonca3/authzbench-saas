## Status

`v1.0-internal` is the internal release-candidate cut. The public-view
readiness fixture is checked with `--allow-incomplete --public-view
--expected-output`; the current fixture reports `v1_ready: false` with
1 unmet gate under honest post-cleanup evidence (release-affecting docs
changed after the pinned `benchmark_source_sha`). This is the
internal/public-view scope only and does not claim external review,
SaaS-provider validation, hosted leaderboard operation,
Harbor/Kaggle/platform acceptance, or third-party submissions. These
tracks are not claimed and are deferred to v2.
